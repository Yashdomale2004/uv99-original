# UV99

> UV99 Glazing is an automotive window-film brand. Its films are engineered to
> block harmful ultraviolet light and reduce infrared heat while keeping the
> view through the glass clear and close to factory appearance.
> Canonical site: https://uv99.in/

This file summarises information that is published on the UV99 website. Figures
are the manufacturer's stated values as shown on the site; the site notes that
published values are QC averages and real-world results vary with the vehicle,
its factory glass and local conditions.

## About

UV99 ("UV99 Glazing") is an automotive glazing brand built around a single
promise: "Harmful rays out, protection in." The films use a non-metallic
nano-ceramic construction that absorbs and re-radiates ultraviolet and infrared
energy while letting visible light pass through with minimal change, so there is
no dye to fade and no metal layer to corrode or interfere with signals.

Brand principles stated on the site: preserve optical clarity (no haze, colour
shift or distortion), label performance honestly (visible light, UV, infrared
and total heat treated as separate measures), keep the result visually
restrained/"factory-fitted", and stand behind the installation with care
guidance, fitment records and written warranty terms.

## Products

The range is five automotive window films. All figures below are taken from the
website's Products page and the UV99+1 page.

### UV99 70
- Positioning: maximum clarity; keeps the character of factory glass
- Visible Light Transmission (VLT): 70%
- UV rejection: 99%
- Total heat rejection: 30%
- Best for: maximum visibility with full UV protection

### UV99 50
- Positioning: balanced protection with more glare control
- Visible Light Transmission (VLT): 47%
- UV rejection: 99%
- Total heat rejection: 35%
- Best for: calmer glare without going dark

### UV PRO 70
- Positioning: clear appearance with meaningful heat relief
- Visible Light Transmission (VLT): 70%
- UV rejection: 99%
- Total heat rejection: 42%
- Best for: a clear look with stronger heat comfort

### UV PRO 50
- Positioning: cooler, calmer cabin with balanced daylight visibility
- Visible Light Transmission (VLT): 51%
- UV rejection: 99%
- Total heat rejection: 50%
- Best for: daytime-heavy driving where glare and heat dominate

### UV99 +1 (flagship)
- Positioning: clear film with UV protection extended across the full UV band to
  400 nm and the strongest cooling in the range
- Visible Light Transmission (VLT): 70%
- UV rejection: 100% (UVA and UVB; full UV block range stated as 280–400 nm)
- Infrared rejection: 89%
- Total heat rejection: 54%
- Construction: multilayer non-metallic nano-ceramic matrix — hard coat
  (scratch resistance), optical polyester base, nano-ceramic core (solar energy
  rejection without metallic signal interference), UVA400 shield (UV filtration
  through the full band to 400 nm)
- Optical: stated haze / optical distortion of about 1%
- Signal-safe: non-metallic, designed not to interfere with phone, GPS or toll
  signals
- Fitment: installed only by certified UV99+1 installers; precision-cut,
  heat-formed and inspected
- Note published on the page: values are QC averages and may vary by batch by
  about ±5%

## Warranty

- Term: 3 years from the date of installation
- Backed by the certified installer who fitted the film
- Covered items (subject to the installer-issued warranty record terms):
  - Bubbling — adhesive or material failure lifting the film from the glass
  - Adhesive failure — peeling, lifting or separation from the mounted surface
  - Discolouration — colour shift or fade beyond the published appearance
    tolerance
  - Performance loss — more than a 10% drop in UV or published infrared
    rejection over the term
- Claims process: handled by the installer from first contact to refitted film;
  customer submits the warranty record number, installation date and daylight
  photos of the affected area
- Resolution: approved claims are put right with replacement film refitted at no
  charge for parts or labour
- Details published at https://uv99.in/warranty.html

## Services

- Supply and professional installation of UV99 automotive window films
- Film selection guidance based on the customer's vehicle, daily route and the
  comfort issue they most want to address (UV, heat, glare, or all three)
- On-site tools to compare the range and preview the look: a comparison /
  "compare & configure" view and a tint simulator on the Products page
- Warranty registration and claims handling through a certified installer
  network
- Dealer / installer partnership enquiries ("Become a Dealer")

Installation notes published on the site: a full vehicle generally takes two to
four hours depending on window count and rear-glass complexity; allow three to
five days for the adhesive to cure fully. Permitted visible-light levels differ
by window position and jurisdiction, and the installer must confirm what is
legal for the vehicle before cutting film.

## Film care

After curing, clean with a soft microfibre cloth and an ammonia-free glass
cleaner. Do not use abrasive pads, blades or scrapers on the filmed face.
